# Research on current SitRep models out there

## Steady
**Pros:**
- Lightweight, distraction-free workspace
- Unifies teams and timezones from tools like slack, github, zoom, google calendar, etc.
- Has useful features like goal story updates, async check-ins, AI-powered feed, and insight about team health
- Everything you need all in one place

**Cons:**
- Not super useful for teams that often meet synchronously
- Fewer third party integrations compared to other platforms
  
## Notion
**Pros:**
- Flexible docs & database system
- The building blocks of a workplace are called "pages" and you have total freedom when it comes to customizing that page
- You can build any workflow you want - wikis, project trackers, roadmaps, CRMs
- AI assistant powerful enough to complete tasks for you
- Solid third-party integrations

**Cons:**
- Tons of different features that are a bit overkill for a small team of 5-10 people
- Because you have total freedom with customizing your workspace it's easy for it to become an unstructured mess
- There's a bit of a learning curve, it's best to get comfortable with it first

## Linear
**Pros:**
- Excellent for software teams with it being keyboard-driven and deeply integrated with GitHub/GitLab
- Great task management features like first-class sprint cycles, full graphs for sub-tasks & depencies, as well as roadmaps & timelines
- Great developer & power features like a keyboard-first design and velocity, burndown charts
- Linear agents to perform tasks for you

**Cons:**
- Very minimal database and docs features - lack of customization
- Not very useful for non-technical teams

# Conclusion
## Feature Priority:
1. Task management is key. Make sure teams are kept up-to-date constantly on progress and task completion of all members. Think timelines, sprint cycles, dependcy graphs, etc.
2. Easy communication with chat/messaging, meeting rooms for voice or video, daily standups, and a way to view people's schedule 
3. Integration with other apps, namely slack and github.
