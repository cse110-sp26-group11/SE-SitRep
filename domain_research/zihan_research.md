# Teamwork OS / SitRep Platform Research

## 1. [Steady](https://runsteady.com)

### Design Observations

Steady has a very clean and minimal interface focused on reducing clutter. The dashboard design emphasizes status updates, async communication, goals, and team awareness rather than overwhelming users with too many menus. Information is grouped in a way that makes updates easy to scan quickly.

### Pros

- Strong support for asynchronous teams
- Centralizes updates, goals, blockers, and team communication
- Integrates with tools like Slack, GitHub, calendars, and Zoom
- AI-generated summaries help reduce repetitive updates
- Clean dashboard makes important information easy to find
- Good for distributed teams working across time zones

### Cons

- May feel too lightweight for teams wanting advanced project management
- Heavy reliance on integrations to unlock full functionality
- AI summaries could miss context or misunderstand situations
- Smaller teams may not need all of the coordination features

### Useful Ideas for Our Project

- Centralized team updates dashboard
- Async standup check-ins
- Blocker visibility
- Simple and uncluttered UI
- Team status summaries

---

## 2. [Notion](https://www.notion.so)

### Design Observations

Notion uses a flexible block-based design system that allows users to build pages however they want. The interface is modern and visually appealing, but because everything is customizable, workspaces can sometimes become messy or inconsistent.

### Pros

- Extremely flexible workspace structure
- Supports documents, databases, calendars, timelines, and task boards
- Strong collaboration tools
- Easy organization with nested pages and folders
- Good visual design with clean typography and layouts
- Works well for both technical and non-technical teams

### Cons

- Large learning curve for new users
- Too much customization can hurt organization
- Requires significant setup before becoming efficient
- Some useful features are limited in free plans
- Can become overwhelming with too many pages and databases

### Useful Ideas for Our Project

- Timeline and dashboard views
- Organized workspace layout
- Meeting notes integration
- Task grouping systems
- Simple but visually polished design

---

## 3. [Linear](https://linear.app)

### Design Observations

Linear focuses heavily on speed and efficiency. The interface is keyboard-driven, fast, and optimized for developers. The design is darker, modern, and very task-oriented. Compared to Notion, Linear feels much more structured and focused.

### Pros

- Excellent task and sprint management
- Very clean developer-focused workflow
- Strong GitHub integration
- Supports subtasks, dependencies, and roadmaps
- Fast and responsive UI
- Great visualization for project progress

### Cons

- Less useful for non-technical users
- Limited document and collaboration features
- More rigid structure compared to Notion
- Focuses mostly on engineering workflows only

### Useful Ideas for Our Project

- Sprint/task workflow system
- Backlog organization
- Subtasks and dependency tracking
- Timeline/progress visualization
- Developer-friendly UI design

# Overall Design Takeaways

After researching these platforms, several common patterns stand out:

## 1. Simplicity Is Important

The best teamwork platforms avoid overwhelming users with too much information at once. Dashboards should surface only the most important updates, blockers, and tasks.

## 2. Visual Progress Tracking Matters

Users should immediately understand project progress through:

- timelines,
- progress bars,
- task completion percentages,
- and sprint tracking systems.

## 3. Async Communication Is Growing

Modern engineering teams rely heavily on async coordination instead of constant meetings. Standups, status updates, and blockers should be easy to submit and easy to review later.

## 4. Integration Reduces Friction

Connecting with tools like GitHub and Slack is important because developers already use them daily.

# Top 3 Features for Our Project

Based on our meeting notes and competitor research, these are the three highest-priority features for our SitRep platform.

---

## 1. Task Dashboard with Timeline & Progress Tracking

This should be the core feature of the platform.

The dashboard should:

- display assigned tasks,
- show deadlines and priorities,
- visualize project completion progress,
- and organize work in a clean timeline format.

This feature is important because almost every researched platform emphasized visibility and organization. Teams need to immediately understand the current project state without searching through multiple pages.

### Why It Matters

- Improves coordination
- Makes sprint progress visible
- Helps identify delayed tasks early
- Reduces confusion about responsibilities

---

## 2. Daily Standups & Blocker Reporting

The platform should allow users to submit quick daily updates about:

- what they completed,
- what they are currently working on,
- and what blockers they have.

Blockers should become highly visible to the team so problems can be solved quickly.

### Why It Matters

- Encourages consistent communication
- Helps teams stay aligned asynchronously
- Prevents hidden delays from growing larger
- Matches Agile workflow requirements from the course

---

## 3. Task Assignment, Roles, & Team Collaboration

Users should be able to:

- create tasks,
- assign tasks to teammates,
- transfer ownership if needed,
- and organize tasks by team or workspace.

Role systems such as Admin and Collaborator are also important for managing permissions.

### Why It Matters

- Creates accountability
- Improves workflow organization
- Supports team scalability
- Makes collaboration easier for larger projects

---

# Final Thoughts

The most successful SitRep platforms focus less on adding countless features and more on reducing coordination friction. A successful product should help teams quickly understand:

- what is happening,
- who is working on what,
- where blockers exist,
- and how close the team is to completing goals.

For our project, the most important goal should be creating a clean and efficient coordination system that supports Agile workflows without becoming overly complicated.
