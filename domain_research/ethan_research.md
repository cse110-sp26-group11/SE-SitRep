# Research: Current SE SitRep Platforms, Pros/Cons, Valuable Features

# App Research

## Standuply
https://standuply.com/

**Pros:**
- Automated Agile processes: team surveys, team meetings (text, voice, video)
- Task trackers, integration with Slack / MS Teams
- Connects to Jira, GitHub, and more for data visualizations (Burndown chart, cumulative flow diagram)
- allows for backlog grooming (backlog management/cleaning) and planning poker (gamified time/effort estimates)
- Daily scrum, retrospective notes, 360 degree feedback surveys, polls
- ChatGPT/Bot integration for better communication insights
- Extra automations: onboarding, food ordering, team mood, etc.

**Cons:**
- More complexity, steeper learning curve
- Only offers Slack and Teams integration
- No native desktop app (Web-based)

## Geekbot
https://geekbot.com/
https://www.capterra.com/p/148076/geekbot/reviews/

**Pros:**
- Scheduled reminders
- Progress updates all bunched together
- Teams and Slack integration
- Free for teams up to 10
- Not SE-specific (capabilities for engineering leads, product owners, CEOs, operations officers, Sales and marketing managers, etc.)
- Team bonding + welfare management
- Polls and surveys (with analytics, templates, AI-assistant support)

**Cons:**
- Over-automation may incentivize quick, thoughtless responses
- More tuned for team updates than project task management
- Potentially confusing/convoluted initial setup

## Vereda AI
https://www.vereda.ai/
Big emphasis on personalization, automation, and a more hands-off approach

**Pros:**
- AI-powered pipeline: standup collection, problem detection, 1:1 prep, goal updating, and performance reviews
- Standup collection: summarizes team-communicated updates, no meetings or engineer sign-up needed (low friction)
- Problem detection: looks for patterns, follows up via personalized DM questions (not just surveys) 
- 1:1 prep: before one-on-ones, gives pre-meeting talking points, ideas, briefs. After, suggests actions for review
- Goal updating: automatically detects progress or any struggles on tasks, and marks them accordingly 
- Performance reviews: Drafts long-term performance reviews to save lots of time on manual research labor
- Quick set-up (under 5 min)
- Slack, Github, Jira integration
- "Built for distributed engineering teams"

**Cons:**
- Only Slack integration (doesn't mention Teams)
- Caters to engineering teams more narrowly
- Less popular, not many reviews online
- AI automation can make errors, misflag sentiments/communication
- Less emphasis on project task management


# Insights/Conclusions

## Takeaways
Good SitReps are frictionless for both set-up and use, though this can come at the tradeoff of customization and personalizability. Traditionally, standardized surveys/polls are used to check on team members, but too much of this rigid automation can feel "forced" on the users' end. AI personalization is an option, but the risk of misinterpretation or oversight still exists.

## Prioritize these features
- Support across all SE teams: Flexible for both asynchronous and synchronous teams, with an emphasis on asynchronous. Keeps teams of all types up to date, with no one left behind. Features that support different roles for engineers, leads, managers, etc.
- Blocker recognition: Identifying team blockers early on saves a lot of headache for scheduling issues that may come later. Using AI to measure team sentiment with personalized questions is a good way to do this, but AI automation can make errors/oversights (tradeoff)
- Flexible integration: Integration with as many platforms as possible (Slack and Teams being the main two). Being able to be its own standalone app helps a lot with team flexibility, should they migrate platforms. 

## Optional, nice to have
- Low setup time: Appealing to prospective users, and overall makes for a smoother experience. Note that this may come with the tradeoff of customizability. If engineers don't have to do much for the initial setup (e.g. no signing in), this is an added bonus
- Pre-meeting notes/briefs: Helps keeps users updated at a glance. Encourages participation even when out-of-the-loop.
- Automated performance review: When done right, can save a LOT of time when summarizing the overall sprint. If you have time to automate anything, this is one of the better ones to do.
- Meetings: Integrated chat / meeting features are nice, but not necessary given integration with other platforms. Don't prioritize this.

## Avoid
- AI Overreliance: while AI automation is nice, the more we rely on it the more we need human vetting to make sure no mistakes/oversights occur. There's probably a happy medium between reducing friction without too much human review.
- Being overbearing: SitReps are supposed to keep teams updated with each other without being too tedious to use. Remember that they go hand-in-hand with project task management and organization.
