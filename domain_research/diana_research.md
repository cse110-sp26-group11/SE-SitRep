# Domain Research: Agile Team Situation Awareness (SE SitRep)
## Core Features (Top 3 Must-Haves)

### 1. Daily Check-In
- A frictionless way to check in asynchronously, answering questions like what did you do today, what are you doing next, and are you blocked?
- Essential for mutual team awareness and smooth workflow
---

### 2.  Blocker Detection 
- A system that identifies and alerts blockers by notifying relevant teammates, suggesting reassignment or assistance.
- This is crucial for detecting lost productivity and addressing it in a timely manner.
---

### 3.  A Team Dashboard 
- A centralized place that shows current tasks and their distribution among team members, status of each member (active, blocked, idle), and etc.
- Clarity should be emphasized over complexity. Team members should be able to get immediate situational awareness without having to spend time looking through the app.
---

## Competitive Platforms

### 1. Jira
**Pros:**
- Strong task structuring (epics, stories, workflows)
- Clear ownership and status tracking
- Deep integration with development pipelines


**Cons:**
- Heavyweight and complex UI
- Not designed for quick daily status updates
- Lacks emotional/contextual awareness


---

### 2. Monday.com
**Pros:**
- Highly visual dashboards
- Customizable workflows
- Easy-to-understand status indicators


**Cons:**
- Requires manual updates (can become stale)
- Over-customization can lead to inconsistency (focus on quality over quantity)
- Limited automation for deeper insights
---

### 3.  ClickUp 
**Pros:**
- Consolidation of multiple tools into one
- Flexible views (list, board, calendar)
- Built-in collaboration features


**Cons:**
- Feature overload leads to cognitive fatigue
- Can feel cluttered and unfocused
- Not optimized for quick situational awareness

---

## Key Takeaway

The main goal of this project is to build a lightweight system that gives teams clear, real-time awareness without adding extra work. Instead of relying on manual updates like traditional tools, SitRep should automatically generate useful insights from team activity, making it both intelligent and low-effort. At the same time, it needs to provide a holistic view of the team by combining task progress, blockers, and overall team status (including AI agents), so anyone can quickly understand what’s happening and where attention is needed.
